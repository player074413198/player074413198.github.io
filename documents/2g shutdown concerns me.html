3G shutdown (2024-2026)

The 3G shutdown (in the uk) started in 2024 and finished up at the start of 2026. When I first heard of it, I was quite concerned but effects turned out to be minimal, the only thing I noticed was slightly lower internet speeds on 3g phones and even then, that doesn't make the experience unusable at all.

There are barely any devices that solely rely on 3G, most of them can also connect to 2G so this shutdown created little to no e-waste.

2G shutdown (2029-2033?)

The 2G shutdown (in the uk) is set to start in 2029 but some networks are beginning tests in certain areas. Promaces have been made to ensure proper 4G/5G coverage of affected areas but as someone who lives in the middle of nowhere I highly doubt that will happen.

The amount of e-waste that will be created by the 2G shutdown will be phenominal, there are phones with 4G but not VoLTE will lose the ability to make calls, there are many people who are perfectly content with their 2g/3g phones.

As someone who likes to collect and use old phones, the shutdown makes me concerned about the future of my hobby as there will be no networks for these phones to connect to unless I make my own network. dosdude1's vid on on hosting a 2g network

The price of feature phones will be draged back a decade as 4g modems cost a considerable ammount more than 2g modems
